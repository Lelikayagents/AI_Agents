# Alert / brief format ("SBL Alert" and, by extension, a "справка")

Derived from two real client Alerts (one on a draft AI regulation law, one on concession/PPP healthcare amendments). An Alert is a deep, single-topic analysis written for a client who needs to understand exactly what changed and what it means for them — the whole point is to demonstrate that the firm caught the change fast and understands it in depth. Where the digest reports, the Alert *advises*.

Use this same shape for a **справка** (a memo answering one specific client legal question) when no dedicated справка template exists yet — treat the question itself as the "development" the Alert would normally open with, and keep the closing recommendations section, since a справка without a practical answer at the end isn't useful to the client.

## Cover slide

- Title: describes the specific change/question, can wrap 2–3 lines, no fixed template sentence (unlike the digest, titles are bespoke per topic — e.g. *"ИИ под контролем: обзор проекта закона о государственном регулировании искусственного интеллекта"*, *"Изменения в законодательство о концессионных соглашениях и соглашениях о ГЧП"*).
- Date line: `DD месяц YYYY` (day, full month name, year — no "г.").

## Opening slide

- First paragraph: what happened, when, who did it, full citation of the instrument with its number, and — if the document will keep referring to it — introduce a short defined name via **«далее – "[Name]»** (e.g. `далее – «Закон об ИИ»`). Once defined, use that exact short name consistently for the rest of the document — same discipline as defined terms in legal-doc-translation.
- Second paragraph (when applicable): effective date, current stage in the legislative process, and near-term outlook (e.g. still open for comment, expected entry into force).
- Then break into thematic subsections.

## Body subsections

- Each subsection has a **bold, unnumbered header** naming the theme (e.g. "Базовые положения", "Новые концепции моделей ИИ", "Права и обязанности лиц при использовании ИИ", "Ответственность в сфере ИИ", "Согласование с ФАС России"). No numbering scheme like the digest's `1.1` — headers are purely topical.
- 1–4 paragraphs per subsection. Lists appear either as plain line breaks (no bullet character) for short enumerations of defined roles/concepts, or as lettered `(a) (b) (c)` / roman `(i) (ii) (iii)` sub-items when the source provision itself has that nested structure — mirror the source's nesting depth exactly, same rule as digests and as legal-doc-translation.
- Where the analysis surfaces a legal nuance, ambiguity, or apparent conflict between provisions (the concession-law Alert spends a full subsection on a possible drafting error creating a retroactivity conflict), walk through it explicitly: state the literal reading, why it might be unintended, and what a careful reading suggests — this analytical depth is the product's actual value, don't compress it away.
- Register here **is** allowed to editorialize, using first-person-plural hedges consistent with the two source Alerts: "мы понимаем, что...", "мы полагаем, что...", "можно предположить, что...", "с практической точки зрения... целесообразно...". This is a deliberate contrast with the digest's neutral third person — don't flatten it out for consistency with the digest register.

## Closing section: "Наши предложения и рекомендации"

Mandatory. Not optional, not a formality — this is the section that turns analysis into value for the client:
- State plainly whether/why the change is relevant to the client's business.
- Name the concrete risk(s) — regulatory, financial, reputational — that follow from inaction, in specific terms tied to the actual provisions discussed above, not generic caution.
- Where useful, suggest a concrete next step (e.g. "проверить, насколько его нормы актуальны для Вашей компании, чтобы своевременно спланировать меры по корректировке существующих бизнес-процессов").

## Footer (every content slide)

Reproduce verbatim on every slide after the cover:
> © ООО «Стоунбридж Лигал», [year]. Сведения и материалы, представленные в настоящем документе, подготовлены исключительно в информационных целях и не являются юридической консультацией или заключением.

Plus the slide number.

## Final slide contact block

Firm phone, email, postal address, and logo — reuse the firm's real contact details, don't invent placeholders:
```

marketing@stonebridgelegal.ru

119017, Россия, Москва
Кадашёвская наб, д. 14, к. 2
```

## Citation discipline

Same as digests: every legal instrument named gets its full official title, number, and date, verbatim, at the point it's introduced — never paraphrase a title or drop a number.
